# Publishing Clips to social networks

doomlearn does not post Clips to TikTok, Instagram or any other network, and it has no share or export feature for them.

## Why this is out of scope

doomlearn is a personal learning tool: Clips are made from the Learner's own reading material for the Learner to study, and that material is often under someone else's copyright. Publishing would also need accounts and credentials on those networks, which [paid-api-and-credentials.md](paid-api-and-credentials.md) rules out.

## Revisit if

The project decides to make Clips for an audience. Remove a section when it is implemented; delete the file when it is empty.
